Whether you're a nurse, a consultant, a contractor, or a coach, your website has one job: to help the right person trust you enough to get in touch. Everything else is secondary.

With that in mind, here are five things every professional website should have.

## 1. A clear statement of who you are and who you help

Not a clever slogan — a plain sentence. "I help new parents find reliable childcare." "I'm a licensed electrician serving the west side." Clarity beats creativity every time.

## 2. A visible way to contact you

Phone, email, or a form — ideally all three, and ideally near the top of the page. If someone has to hunt for your contact details, they'll leave.

## 3. Proof you're real and qualified

Credentials, testimonials, a photo, a license number, a short bio. Trust signals matter more than design flourishes.

## 4. A description of your services

Not an exhaustive list — a clear one. What do you do, who is it for, and what happens next?

## 5. A design that doesn't get in the way

Fast, clean, readable on a phone. Most of your visitors will arrive on mobile. If your site looks bad there, nothing else matters.

The good news: you don't need to design any of this from scratch. The structure is well understood, and ready-made professional websites can handle all five points out of the box.
