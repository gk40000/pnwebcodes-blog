It's tempting to run everything through social media. The platforms are free, the audience is already there, and posting feels easy. But relying on social media alone is a bit like building a house on rented land.

You don't control the algorithm. You don't own the follower list. And if the platform changes its rules tomorrow — which it does — your reach can vanish overnight.

A website is different. It's yours. It doesn't compete with ads for attention. It doesn't disappear because you posted at the wrong time. It works quietly in the background, twenty-four hours a day, turning curious visitors into clients.

## Three things a website does that social media can't

**It builds credibility.** Anyone can make a social profile. A polished, professional website signals that you take your work seriously.

**It gives people a place to land.** Social posts scroll by. A website gives potential clients a place to slow down, read about what you do, and decide if you're the right fit.

**It turns strangers into leads.** A contact form. A booking link. An email signup. These simple tools convert interest into action in a way a social post rarely does.

## The bottom line

Social media is brilliant for discovery. But discovery without a destination is just noise.
