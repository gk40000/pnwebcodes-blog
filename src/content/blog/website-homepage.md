The homepage is the most important page on your site. It has one job: help the right visitor decide to stay. Everything else is secondary.

## Above the fold

Before anyone scrolls, they should see three things: who you are, who you help, and how to contact you. Plain language. No slogans that require decoding.

## Trust signals

A photo. A credential. A testimonial. A logo. Small proof that you're real and qualified. These reassure visitors in seconds.

## Services

A short description of what you do. Not a full list — just enough that someone knows whether you're relevant to them.

## A clear next step

Book a call. Send a message. Request a quote. Whatever the next step is, make it obvious and easy.

## What to leave off

Long paragraphs about your journey. Animated intros that delay the page. Vague taglines. Anything that makes the visitor work harder than they need to.

Keep it clear, keep it fast, and keep the focus on the visitor — not on you.
