---

At a glance, a Facebook page and a website look similar. Both have a name, a photo, some text, and a way to contact you. But they're not the same tool — and the differences matter.

## Ownership

Your Facebook page belongs to Facebook. Your website belongs to you. That single difference shapes everything else.

## Control

On Facebook, you follow their rules. Design options are limited. Features change without notice. On your own website, you decide how it looks, what it says, and what it does.

## Discovery

Facebook pages are hard to find unless someone already knows your name. Websites are indexed by Google and can be found by people searching for what you offer — often before they know who you are.

## Credibility

A Facebook page says "I'm here." A website says "I'm a professional." For anyone selling expertise, that distinction is worth the effort.

## Do you need both?

Yes — ideally. Use social media to be discovered. Use your website to be chosen. They do different jobs, and they work best together.
