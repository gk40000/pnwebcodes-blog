title: "3 Website Mistakes Small Businesses Should Avoid"
description: "Hidden contact info, slow load times, and designs that fight the content. Three mistakes that quietly cost you customers."
date: 2026-10-07
---

Most small business websites fail for the same three reasons. None of them are about design taste. All of them are about clarity and speed.

## 1. Making contact information hard to find

If someone has to scroll, click, or guess how to reach you, they won't. Your phone number, email, or contact form should be visible immediately — ideally in the header and again near the bottom of every page.

## 2. Letting the site load slowly

Huge images, too many fonts, and bloated scripts all slow a site down. Every extra second of load time loses visitors. Aim for fast, clean, lightweight pages — especially on mobile.

## 3. Letting the design fight the content

Animated backgrounds, hard-to-read fonts, and clever layouts might look impressive — but they get in the way of the message. Your content should be the star. The design should support it, not compete with it.

## The fix

Make contact obvious. Make the site fast. Make the content easy to read. Do those three things well, and your website will outperform most of your competitors — regardless of budget.
