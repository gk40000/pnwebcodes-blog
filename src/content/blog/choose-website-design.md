Design isn't just aesthetic. It communicates who you are before anyone reads a word. Choosing the right design for your profession is about matching the expectations your clients already have.

## Modern

Clean lines, generous white space, subtle animation. Modern designs suit consultants, coaches, tech professionals, and anyone whose work feels contemporary.

## Classic

Traditional layouts, serif fonts, structured sections. Classic designs suit accountants, lawyers, healthcare professionals, and anyone whose clients value stability and trust.

## Bold

Strong colours, big type, confident sections. Bold designs suit personal trainers, creative professionals, contractors, and anyone whose work benefits from visible energy.

## How to decide

Ask one question: what do my clients expect from me? If they expect calm reliability, choose Classic. If they expect modern clarity, choose Modern. If they expect energy and confidence, choose Bold.
