Social media feels like enough. It's where your customers are. It's where you get attention. So why bother with a website?

Because social media isn't yours. And three specific risks make that clear.

## 1. You don't control the algorithm

Every platform decides who sees your posts. Reach that was once free now costs money. Accounts that once grew quickly now plateau. You're building on land someone else owns — and they can change the rules any time.

## 2. You don't own your audience

Your followers aren't really yours. If your account is restricted or removed, you lose access to every one of them. A website's email list, by contrast, belongs to you. Nobody can take it away.

## 3. You can't build a serious brand on rented space

Professional services — healthcare, consulting, coaching, trades — run on trust. Trust needs a home. A website is that home. It's where credentials live, where testimonials sit, and where a potential client decides you're worth contacting.

## What to do instead

Don't abandon social media. Use it for discovery. Then give every interested person somewhere to land — a website you own, control, and can update on your own terms.
